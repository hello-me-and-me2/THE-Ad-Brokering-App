About Us

A Letter From Our Team

THE Ad Brokering App · Founded 2026

Too many great partnerships fall apart in the DMs — vague offers, ghosted messages, and terms nobody wrote down. We started THE Ad Brokering App to make influencer and business deals feel less like a scramble and more like a conversation between people who trust each other.

Our Story

We built THE Ad Brokering App as the home base where brands and creators can find each other, talk it through, and land on a deal that actually works for both sides — plainly, honestly, and without the back-channel guesswork.

What We Stand For

  • Fair deals, plainly stated

    Every offer and counter-offer lives in the open, so both sides always know where things stand.

  • Real relationships

    We're building a network of people, not just accounts — the kind of partnerships that last past one campaign.

  • Small business, big heart

    We're a growing team that still answers our own emails. If you need us, we're here.

Warmly,
