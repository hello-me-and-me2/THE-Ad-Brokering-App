Help

Quick answers, and a way to reach us.

Common questions

How do deals work here?

Find someone in The Network or Outreach, send a request to message, then negotiate in Messaging & Negotiation. Send offers and counter-offers, accept one, and draft a contract from the accepted terms. Both sides sign, and the contract's owner can record when it's paid.

Does THE INTROductory handle payments?

No. Payment happens between you and the other party. You can mark a signed contract as paid so you have a record, but no money moves through the app.

How many videos can I post?

Creator accounts get three video slots, each long-form or short-form. Add a link to a YouTube, Vimeo, or TikTok video, or a direct video file, in the Outreach tab.

What's the difference between Business Outreach and Campaign Briefs?

Outreach posts are open invitations — sponsorship calls, events, networking — where people tap "I'm in". A campaign brief is a specific job with a budget and deliverables, and creators apply with a pitch that the business accepts or declines.

How do I get verified?

Apply from your Profile with a short reason. An admin reviews it, and you'll get a notification with the decision.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile (or the video or post) and use Report or Block. Reports go to an admin. Blocking hides you and them from each other.

Are electronic signatures legally binding?

This app is not legal advice, and signing a contract here does not by itself make it legally binding. Talk to a lawyer for anything important. See the Terms.

How do I delete my account or download my data?

Both are in Settings.
