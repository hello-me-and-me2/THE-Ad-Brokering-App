Privacy Policy

Last updated January 2026

The short version: this is a client-side demo, so there's nothing to collect. Everything you type stays on your own device. Here's the longer version, for completeness.

What We Collect

Nothing. THE INTROductory has no backend server and no database. There's no sign-up email list, no analytics tracking, and no third party ever receives anything you type into this demo.

Where Your Data Lives

  • Your profile, messages, contracts, and preferences are saved in your browser's local storage, under this site's origin only.
  • That data never leaves your browser — it isn't sent to a server, logged, or shared.
  • It stays until you clear your browser's site data, after which it's gone for good.

Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies. The handful of settings it does remember (like whether you've signed in, or light/dark mode) are stored the same way as everything else — in local storage, on your device only.

Third Parties

The only outside requests this page makes are for web fonts (Google Fonts). No user data is sent as part of those requests.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to andre.jacob.anderson@gmail.com.
